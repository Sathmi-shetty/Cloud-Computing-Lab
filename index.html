CLOUDS - CSE Student Association

CLOUDS (Computer Leaders Official United Development Society) is the official student association of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management. It promotes technical excellence through workshops, hackathons, coding competitions, and collaborative learning activities to bridge the gap between academics and industry.
